Installation

Install the IPA using AltStore on your iPhone or iPad.

1

Download

Download the latest KOReader-iOS.ipa from the GitHub Release.

2

Open AltStore

Open AltStore on your iPhone or iPad and add the downloaded IPA.

3

Install

Select KOReader and wait for AltStore to finish the installation.

Important: This is a community iOS/iPadOS build of KOReader. It is not distributed through the Apple App Store.

Installation through a free Apple Developer account may require periodic refreshing.
